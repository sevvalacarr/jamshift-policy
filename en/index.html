Bumpshift — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026

Bumpshift (the “game”) is a free puzzle game for Android and iOS offered by JamShift Games. This policy explains how the game handles your data.

Summary

  • We do not ask for or collect personal information such as your name, email or phone number. There are no accounts.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.
  • The game contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools. The game’s own code does not connect to the internet.
  • The game shows Google AdMob ads. For ads, Google collects and processes certain data from your device (see “Ads”).

Data stored on your device

So that you can continue where you left off, the game stores the following only on your device, in the app’s private folder:

  • unlocked levels, stars earned and best move counts;
  • Daily Puzzle results and streak;
  • sound, music and vibration preferences;
  • whether you have seen the tutorial.

This data is not sent to us or to any third party. It is also excluded from backups: Android auto backup and device-to-device transfer, and iCloud and computer backups on iOS. If you delete the game or clear its data, your progress cannot be restored.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Rewarded ads: Shown only if you choose to watch one, to earn an extra hint after your free hints are used. Watching is never required.
  • Interstitial ads: Shown only between levels, and never during the first five levels.

There are no banner ads and no in-app purchases.

Ads are served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. According to Google’s documentation, the SDK may collect the following and send it to Google:

  • IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location);
  • device identifiers: on Android, the advertising ID and app set ID; on iOS, app- or developer-scoped identifiers and, only if you allow it, the advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • ads viewed and app interactions (app launches, ad views and taps);
  • diagnostics (crash logs and performance information).

Google processes this data for ad delivery and measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Details: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent

Before requesting ads, the game checks your consent status with Google’s consent tool (User Messaging Platform), which connects to Google for this check. Where consent is required in your region (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google’s consent form may be shown. The ads SDK is started only when this tool allows ads to be requested, and this is checked again before every ad request. Where required in your region, a “Privacy options” link appears in the game’s Sound Settings panel, where you can change your choice.

iOS

The advertising identifier (IDFA) can only be accessed if you allow it in the iOS tracking permission prompt. If you do not allow it, or the prompt is not shown, the IDFA is not used; ads are still shown and the whole game remains playable. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files. On Android, the internet and advertising ID permissions are added by the ads SDK. The game works offline; only ads are not shown.

Children

Bumpshift is designed for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, please contact us.

Deleting your data

  • Android: Settings → Apps → Bumpshift → Storage → “Clear data”, or uninstall the game.
  • iOS: Delete the game from your device.

We do not hold any data that can be linked to you. For data processed by Google for ads, you can use Google’s tools: myaccount.google.com.

Data controller

JamShift Games is a brand name, not a legal entity. The data controller is the independent developer who publishes the game under this brand: Şevval Açar. Contact: jamshift.support@gmail.com

For advertising data, Google is also a controller under its own privacy policy.

Legal basis and international transfers

Game data stored on your device is used only to run the game and never leaves your device. Processing for advertising relies, where required, on the consent you give through Google’s consent tool, and otherwise on the legal bases described in Google’s policies. Google may transfer this data to servers outside Türkiye and the European Economic Area.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, under the GDPR or Türkiye’s Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698, Art. 11) you have the right to access, rectify or erase your data, to object to processing and to withdraw your consent. Since we hold no data that can be linked to you, you can use Google’s tools for advertising data or contact us. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority).
